This synth uses the Source-Filter model of speech. A raw sound (Source) is shaped by resonances (Filters) to create vocal sounds.

The Pad: Maps F1 (height) and F2 (backness). Dragging morphs between vowel shapes.

Formants (F1, F2, F3): These are resonant peaks created by the shape of the vocal tract. F1/F2 are the primary identifiers for vowels.

  • Pitch: The fundamental frequency of the 'glottal source' (the buzz of the vocal folds).
  • BW (Bandwidth): The 'width' of the resonance. Narrower bandwidths sound more like a whistle or a pure tone; wider sounds more natural.
  • Scale: Simulates a longer or shorter vocal tract (shifting all formants together). This makes the voice sound larger/deeper or smaller/brighter.
